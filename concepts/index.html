What to judge

  • Tone. Does it sound like the project: measured, evidence-led, not promotional?
  • First screen. Does the claim land before the reader scrolls?
  • Evidence. Are the pilot numbers prominent enough to be useful, and caveated enough to be honest?
  • Growth. Could a news or publications section be added without redrawing the page?
  • Mobile. The audience will often arrive from a link on a phone.

All three share the same content and the same caveats. Pick on presentation. If none fit, describe what should change and we will build a fourth.
